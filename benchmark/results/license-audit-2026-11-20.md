## Reuse conditions

For CC BY material, preserve:

1. Article title and author attribution.
2. DOI and publisher/source URL.
3. A link to the applicable license.
4. An indication if the PDF or any material was modified.
5. No implication that the authors or publisher endorse PaperSignal.

The audit does **not** automatically clear third-party figures, photos, datasets or other material separately credited in an article. Those items may have distinct rights and must be checked individually before they are redistributed or embedded in a public release.

## Disposition

- Article-level license check: **complete for all ten supplied PDFs**.
- Third-party-material exception check: not completed for every individual figure/table; do not assume all embedded third-party material is covered by the article's CC BY license.
- This is a document-level rights check, not legal advice. Re-check if a publisher PDF is replaced or a different version is used.
